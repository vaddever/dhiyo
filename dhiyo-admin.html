Publish your changes

  1. Download the updated file below.
  2. Open your GitHub repository and choose Add file → Upload files.
  3. Drop in products.json (same folder as the shop page) and commit.
  4. Wait a minute or two, then refresh the shop. Your changes are live.

Backup & restore

Your edits are saved automatically in this browser until you publish. They are not live until you upload the file.
